Privacy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Streety is a parking map. You don't need an account, there are no ads, and we don't sell or share your information with anyone for marketing. This page explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

Your location

If you tap the location button, use drive mode, or tap "I parked here", your phone shares your location with the Streety app so it can show where you are and where to park. Streety uses it on your phone and doesn't send it to our servers. You can turn location access off in your phone's settings at any time.

Things saved on your phone

Your settings ("My car", your permit area, mobility permit), saved places, where you parked, and any sign photos you read are stored on your phone only (in the browser's storage). Clearing the site's data or your browser data removes them.

Reports and sign readings you send

When you send a report ("Report a wrong sign") or choose to share a sign reading, we receive what you send: the street or car park, its position on the map, your note, the sign wording, and a photo if you add one. We use these only to fix and improve the map. They are stored with our hosting provider, Cloudflare. Please don't include people's faces or number plates in photos. We don't ask for your name or contact details.

Services the app uses

  • Map tiles come from OpenFreeMap, and street photos of signs (when shown) from Mapillary. Like any website, these services see your internet address and which part of the map is loading.
  • Search sends what you type to the Photon search service (komoot) to find places.
  • Visitor counts: we may use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page visits without cookies and without tracking you across websites.
  • Street View only opens Google if you tap the Street View link.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Streety uses them only to remind you to move your car. They're created on your phone.

Children

Streety is made for drivers and isn't directed at children.

Changes and questions

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. For questions, use "Report a wrong sign" on any street (choose "Something else") or the report button on a car park.
