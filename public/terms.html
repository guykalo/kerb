What Streety is

Streety shows parking information on a map: time limits, meter prices, car park prices, permit areas and likely fines. It is built from councils' and governments' open data, published fee lists, OpenStreetMap and readings people share. By using Streety you agree to these terms.

Information may be wrong or out of date

Councils change signs, prices and rules, and their published data can be incomplete, late or wrong. Streety labels its estimates ("est.", "usually", "probably free"), but any information in the app can be wrong. Car park prices can change at any time; the board at the entrance is the price you pay. Fine amounts are from published government data and change each year.

Driving

Don't use your phone while driving unless it's in a mounted cradle and you're allowed to under your state's road rules. Set up drive mode before you start driving, and let the voice do the talking. Never stop where it isn't safe or legal to check the app.

Reports and sign readings

When you send a report or share a sign reading, you let Streety use it to improve the map for everyone. Only send things you're happy to share, and don't include people's faces or number plates.
